BIOGRAPHY

TEA is a breath of fresh air to the creative scene. The UK based producer, singer, and songwriter is born of movement and meaning, known not to conform to trends but to quietly adopt them.

His style is rooted in simplicity, freedom, and emotional depth, embodying a centred yet spirited energy.

Using his sound, he draws on his evolving experience and understanding of life to promote Truth, Expression, and Acceptance.
